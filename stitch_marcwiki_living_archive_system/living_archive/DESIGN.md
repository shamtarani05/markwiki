## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an intellectual, cinematic, and slightly mysterious presence. It reimagines digital knowledge archives not as static, dry directories, but as living, evolving intellectual spaces. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a hybrid of **High-Contrast Editorial Typography** and **Dark Glassmorphic Modernism**. It balances razor-sharp structural discipline with atmospheric illumination. Interactions feel deliberate, weightless, and resonant—evoking the gravitas of a classified obsidian catalog illuminated by violet radiation. Every view treats knowledge as precious artifact: razor-thin dividers, disciplined micro-metadata, deep tonal layers, and luminous accent glows guide the reader through vast amounts of information without sensory fatigue.

## Colors
The palette leverages deep cosmic charcoals and obsidians to establish an infinite canvas, punctuated by focused lilac and violet glows.

- **Background Canvas (`#0B0B0F`)**: Deep cosmic charcoal. Absorbs light and provides infinite negative space.
- **Surface Layers**:
  - Base Surface (`#121218`): Dark obsidian for persistent containers, side panels, and resting card backdrops.
  - Surface Elevated (`#181820`): Modals, floating sheets, elevated cards, and active menus.
  - Soft Surface (`#20202A`): Interactive pill badges, button hover states, and input field backgrounds.
- **Accents**:
  - Primary Violet (`#8B5CF6`): High-energy interactive highlights, primary triggers, and key brand marks.
  - Accent Bright (`#A78BFA`): Lilac glow used for focus boundaries, text links on hover, and vibrant edge highlights.
  - Accent Soft (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.14)`): Ambient backdrops, selection states, and tag fills.
- **Text & Editorial Hierarchy**:
  - Primary Text (`#F5F3EF`): Warm off-white, eliminating blinding pure white glare for effortless long-form reading.
  - Secondary Text (`#A7A5AE`): Cool silver for descriptive body prose, subtitles, and secondary navigation.
  - Muted Text (`#706F78`): Slate for metadata, numbered catalog indexes, and timestamps.
- **Borders & Dividers**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)` provides razor-thin delineations without visually segmenting layouts aggressively.
- **Functional Semantics**: Success (`#35C98A`), Warning (`#F2B84B`), Danger (`#EF6461`).

## Typography
The typographic identity relies on a high-contrast dialogue between classical literary elegance and high-precision technical modernism.

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy (`Newsreader`)**: Imbues the archive with historical weight and literary authority. Featuring tight letter spacing and high stroke contrast, headers capture editorial poise.
- **Interface & Reading Prose (`Geist`)**: Used for all functional UI elements, micro-labels, and long-form body content. Set with an intentional `1.65` line height (`body-editorial`) to guarantee sustained focus during in-depth research.
- **Micro-Caps Metadata (`label-caps`)**: Always uppercase with widened letter-spacing (`0.12em`) to cleanly label document classifications, index numbers, access tiers, and taxonomic tags.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for structural balance and deep content reading.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (under 768px; 4 columns; margin: `1.25rem`), Tablet (768px–1199px; 8 columns; margin: `2rem`), Desktop (1200px+; 12 columns; margin: `4rem`; max structural inner width: `1440px`).
- **Reading Well**: For primary editorial content, constrain the reading container to a focused width of `720px–780px` (centered or flanked by taxonomy side rails) to maintain an optimal 65–75 character measure per line.
- **Rhythm**: Layouts employ generous vertical padding (`space-2xl`) between narrative sections, reinforced by 1px dividers that fade to transparent at container boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the system is not simulated with muddy dropshadows, but rendered through luminous atmospheric layers, surface tonal elevation, and frosted light filters.

- **Base Layer (Ground)**: `#0B0B0F` canvas overlaid with subtle radial lighting: `radial-gradient(ellipse 80% 50% at 50% -20%, rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08), transparent)`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: `#121218` surface bordered with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered & Focused States)**: `#181820` surface with faint lilac rim-light (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08), 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`).
- **Level 3 (Command Palette & Overlays)**: Background `#181820` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an ambient aura: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 0 1px rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.25)`.
- **Dividers**: Always `1px` stroke using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)` or gradient masking (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12), transparent)`).

## Shapes
The visual identity demands crisp, architectural lines. Elements adopt a refined, softly clipped structure (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Default UI (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Buttons, input fields, and small badges remain sharp and precise.
- **Cards & Modals (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Medium containers soften the edges just enough to prevent visual harshness while sustaining technical clarity.
- **Floating Overlays & Chips (`0.75rem` / `12px`)**: Applied selectively to floating navigation bars and nested category pills.
- **Pill Shape (`9999px`)**: Exclusively reserved for status indicator pips, user avatars, and live search tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#8B5CF6`, text `#F5F3EF`, font `label-caps` or `body-sm` bold. Subtle top highlight border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`. On hover: `#A78BFA` with glow `0 0 20px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Background transparent, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)`, text `#A7A5AE`. On hover: Background `#181820`, text `#F5F3EF`, border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.

### Command Palette (Search Overlay)
- Floating dialog with backdrop blur (`24px`) on `#121218/90`.
- Input field has no internal border; uses `20px` typography (`headline-sm`) with `#F5F3EF` text and `#706F78` placeholder.
- Bottom actions bar displays keyboard hints (`⌘K`, `ESC`, `ENTER`) styled as micro-caps in `#20202A` badge containers.

### Editorial Discovery Cards
- Background `#121218`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)`.
- Features a subtle violet ambient edge glow on hover. Top margin includes category micro-caps and archive index numerals (e.g., `// VOL. 04`) set in `Muted Text`.
- Overlay images feature a cinematic gradient mask: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(18, 18, 24, 0) 40%, #121218 100%)`.

### Numbered Editorial Discovery Lists
- Compact row items separated by 1px bottom borders.
- Leading item index: Two-digit tabular numeral (e.g., `01`, `02`) in `Newsreader` italic, colored `#706F78`, shifting to `#A78BFA` on row hover.
- Title transitions seamlessly from `#A7A5AE` to `#F5F3EF` with a 4px horizontal slide on interaction.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs**: Background `#121218`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)`, text `#F5F3EF`. Focus state triggers border `1px solid #8B5CF6` and inset shadow `0 0 0 1px #8B5CF6`.
- **Checkboxes**: 16x16px squares with 3px radius. Unchecked: `#181820` border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`. Checked: `#8B5CF6` with white vector checkmark.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Height: 24px. Padding: 0 8px.
- Background: `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`, border: `1px solid rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.25)`, text: `#A78BFA`, font: `label-caps`.

### Responsive Chrome & Navigation
- Top app bar: Semi-translucent `#0B0B0F` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- Bottom border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Integrated live status pulse: 6px circular pip in `#35C98A` with a 2-second radiating breath animation.

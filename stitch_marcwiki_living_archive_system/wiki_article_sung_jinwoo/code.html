SECTION 01 // ARCHIVE FOUNDATION

Overview

Sung Jinwoo (Korean: 성진우) is the central protagonist of Solo Leveling. Initially notorious across the Korean Hunter Association as the "World's Weakest Hunter" (an infamous E-Rank), he experienced a miraculous phenomenon known as the Second Awakening within the subterranean Double Dungeon of the D-Rank Cartenon Temple.

“
I shall protect my family, even if all the gods of this world become my enemy.

Following his rebirth as the sole avatar of The System created by the Architect, Jinwoo possessed an unprecedented capacity denied to all other hunters: the ability to infinitely level up, expand his status parameters, and extract the deceased spirits of foes to form an immortal sovereign regiment known as the Shadow Army.

SECTION 02 // CHRONOLOGICAL RECORD

Biography & Origins

Born to Hunter Sung Il-Hwan and Park Kyung-Hye, Jinwoo was thrust into financial desperation after his father's sudden disappearance inside an unknown Blue Gate, and his mother's succumbing to the fatal magical affliction known as Eternal Slumber. To pay hospital tariffs and secure academic funds for his sister Sung Jinah, he enlisted in low-tier raiding parties despite suffering near-lethal injuries in almost every standard E-Rank excursion.

The Cartenon Temple Incident TIMELINE: YEAR 2017

PHASE 01 • THE DOUBLE GATE

Discovery of the Subterranean Lair

Following the clearance of a standard D-Rank dungeon, a secondary chamber opening was identified. The raiding team voted 6 to 5 to proceed inward, leading directly to the statue circle of Cartenon Temple.

PHASE 02 • THE THREE COMMANDMENTS

The God Statue Slaughter

The colossal God Statue closed the doors and unleashed incinerating laser gazes. Jinwoo deciphered the commandments: "Worship God", "Praise God", and "Prove Your Faith", minimizing casualties through deductive intellect.

PHASE 03 • THE FINAL SACRIFICE

Designation of Player 00

Abandoned on the central sacrificial pedestal with a severed leg, Jinwoo was chosen by the System mere milliseconds prior to terminal damage. He woke three days later in a hospital ward, fully regenerated.

SECTION 03 // ARCHITECTURAL INTERFACE

Dual Awakening & System Architecture

The mechanism controlling Jinwoo was a bespoke gamified operating environment manufactured by the Architect under contract with the original Shadow Monarch, Ashborn. Unlike ordinary humans whose magical threshold is hard-coded at their initial awakening, Jinwoo received programmatic quest regimens, penalty survival rooms, and dynamic stat allocation points.

SECTION 05 // LEGION ORDNANCE

The Shadow Army Hierarchy

The Shadow Army operates under strict feudal tiering governed by Jinwoo's sovereign authority. Each rank dictates autonomous decision-making capability, vocal ability, and regenerative speed.

GRAND MARSHAL

Bellion

Original servant of Ashborn born from the Fruit of the World Tree. Wields a colossal centipede chain sword capable of cleaving dimensional walls.

MARSHAL

Beru (Ant King)

Extracted from the S-Rank Jeju Island Ant Queen offspring. Retains predatory gluttony skill, supersonic flight, and extreme loyalty to the Monarch family.

MARSHAL

Blood-Red Igris

Jinwoo's inaugural elite knight, extracted in the Job Change Quest. Master of dual longswords, lightning telekinesis, and chivalric discipline.
